Delete Your Unentangle Account

We respect your privacy. You have full control over your personal data and account.

Option 1: From within the Unentangle App (Instant)

  1. Open the Unentangle app.
  2. Tap the Settings icon.
  3. Scroll down to Account Deletion / Data Purge.
  4. Confirm your request. Your account enters a 24-hour grace period before permanent deletion.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, email our support team with your registered email address to request complete account purging:

Email Deletion Request
What data is deleted: Your account profile, authentication credentials, attachment assessment quiz scores, personal reflection journal entries, and boundary blueprints will be permanently deleted from our servers.
